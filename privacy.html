1. Who we are

Artisan Roofing Limited is the controller of your personal information. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

We take care to handle your information properly and endeavour to keep to everything in this notice. If we ever get something wrong, please tell us and we will put it right as quickly as we can.

Artisan Roofing Limited, Unit 1, Bankhead Workspace, Bankhead Terrace, Edinburgh, EH11 4DY. Registered in Scotland, company number SC252833.

Data protection contact: Cyrille Piot, Director. Email cpo@artiroof.co.uk, telephone 0131 453 5442.

2. The information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us (as a customer, a homeowner, a main contractor, an architect, a supplier or a subcontractor), we may hold:

  • Contact details: name, job title, company, postal address, email address and telephone numbers.
  • Project and property information: the site address, drawings, specifications, our measurements, 3D models, quotes, and photographs of the property and of the work.
  • Communications: emails, letters, messages and notes of calls and meetings about a project.
  • Financial information: invoices, payments, payment terms, retentions and the bank details needed to pay or be paid.
  • Credit and identity checks: the results of checks made through a credit reference agency (see section 6).
  • Marketing preferences: whether you have agreed to receive news and offers from us.

We do not ask for, and do not normally hold, special category information (such as health, religion or ethnic origin).

3. Where we get it from

  • Directly from you, when you ask for a quote, accept one, or work with us.
  • From the people who bring us into a project: main contractors, architects, engineers, surveyors and property managers. This can include a homeowner's name and address on drawings or tender documents.
  • From credit reference agencies (Creditsafe and its data partner TransUnion).
  • From public sources, such as Companies House and maps used to locate a site.

5. Automated decision making and profiling

We may use automated systems and tools to support certain business processes, such as risk assessment, fraud prevention, affordability checks, identity verification, or record management. These tools may analyse personal data using predefined criteria or rules to generate indicators, scores, or recommendations. However, we do not make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on individuals based solely on automated processing. Any such decisions are subject to meaningful human review.

The use of these tools may influence the speed or level of review applied to an application or request, but individuals will not be subject to automatic rejection or adverse decisions without human involvement.

6. Credit and affordability checks

To help us assess applications, prevent fraud, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations, we may obtain information about you from credit reference agencies (CRAs). We obtain this information via Creditsafe, which uses its data partner TransUnion to supply consumer credit and identity data.

Creditsafe Business Solutions Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FCA Firm Reference Number: 742313. TransUnion International UK Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FCA Firm Reference Number: 737740.

The information we receive may include data relating to your identity, credit commitments, payment history, and public record information. This data is used solely for legitimate business purposes, including creditworthiness assessment, identity verification, and fraud prevention, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Further information about how Creditsafe and TransUnion process your personal data can be found in their respective privacy notices: Creditsafe Privacy / Transparency Notice (Transparency Notice | Customers & Suppliers, on the Creditsafe website), and TransUnion Bureau Privacy Notice: https://www.transunion.co.uk/legal/privacy-centre/pc-bureau

7. Who we share it with

We endeavour to share only what is needed, and only with:

  • Subcontractors, suppliers and manufacturers working on your project.
  • The main contractor, architect, engineer or surveyor on the same project.
  • Our accountant and bookkeeper.
  • Credit reference agencies (Creditsafe / TransUnion).
  • Our IT and software providers, including our email provider (Google) and accounting software (QuickBooks, Intuit), and the companies who maintain and back up our systems.
  • Banks and payment providers, insurers, solicitors and debt collection agencies, when needed.
  • HMRC, the courts, the police or other authorities when the law requires it.

We ask third parties who handle information for us to keep it secure and to use it only for the purpose we give them.

Photographs of our work. We like to show finished projects on our website and social media. We always endeavour to ask for your permission first, and to avoid publishing your name, the exact address of the property or any other personal information unless you want us to. If something has been published that you are not happy with, or you change your mind, please tell us and we will remove it from our website and our social media pages as soon as possible (we cannot control copies other people may already have shared).

Use of our project records for research and technology development. Our records of past projects (the drawings, 3D models, measurements, quantities and prices we prepared) are a valuable record of how roofing and cladding work is designed and priced. We may make these records available, or license them, to organisations that develop software and artificial intelligence tools for the construction industry, or use them for research. Before doing so we take care to remove names, contact details, addresses and any other details that could identify you or your home, and we do not intend to include information that identifies you without your consent. If you find anything that could identify you, or you would like your project left out, please tell us (see section 11) and we will deal with it as soon as possible.

We do not sell or rent your personal information (your name, contact details or address) for anyone else's marketing.

8. Transfers outside the UK

Most of our information is held in the UK. Some of our service providers (for example our email and accounting software providers) may store or process information outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK-US Data Bridge, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

9. How long we keep it

We keep records of our projects for as long as they may be needed: to honour any guarantee or warranty that applies to the work, to carry out repairs or future work on the same building, and to deal with any claim. Building work can be questioned many years after it is finished, so these records are kept for the long term.

We keep accounting and tax records for at least the period the law requires (currently 6 years), and may keep them longer as part of our business records.

We endeavour to review what we hold regularly. Where personal details are no longer needed, for example on quotes that did not go ahead, we delete them or remove them, while keeping the technical record of the work. Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe.

10. Keeping it secure

We endeavour to protect personal information with sensible measures, such as password-protected systems, access limited to people who need it, regular backups and secure disposal of paper (shredding). No system is completely secure. If there is a data breach that puts your information at risk, we will act quickly to limit any harm and will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) where the law requires.

11. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your information where there is no good reason to keep it.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Objection: object to us using your information where we rely on legitimate interests, and at any time to direct marketing.
  • Data portability: ask for information you gave us to be passed to you or another organisation.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Some rights have exemptions, so they may not apply in every case. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 1. There is normally no charge. As the law requires, we will reply without undue delay and within one month (this can be extended in some cases), and may need to confirm your identity first.

12. Is providing personal data required? (statutory or contractual)

Providing personal data is mainly a contractual requirement and, in some cases, a statutory (legal) requirement, for example for accounting, tax and fraud prevention. We need it to quote, enter into and carry out contracts with customers, suppliers and subcontractors, to manage accounts and invoice, to verify identity and prevent fraud, and to meet our legal obligations.

If you choose not to provide it, we may be unable to enter into a contract with you, carry out the work or provide our services, or complete the checks we need to make, and our services may be delayed, restricted or declined.

Information for optional purposes, such as marketing, is never required, and you can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the goods or services you receive from us.

13. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first (section 1) and we will look into it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): helpline 0303 123 1113, website https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. ICO Head Office: 4th Floor, No. 3 Circle Square, 5 Hawkshaw Street, Manchester, M1 7BL.

14. Changes to this notice

We review this notice from time to time and will publish any changes. This version is dated 19/11/2026.

Using our website

When you use our contact form we receive the details you enter: your name, city, email address, telephone and fax numbers, and your message. We use them to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to work with us, to provide our quotation and services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Your enquiry is delivered to our email inbox by our email sending provider, Resend, and stored in our email account with Google Workspace. Both act on our instructions and are bound to protect your information.

Our web server briefly records your IP address to protect the form against spam and abuse. IP addresses used for this are discarded within an hour; server logs are kept for a short period for security.

This website does not use cookies or any tracking or analytics tools.
